# Mozio

> Mozio is a global ground transportation booking platform connecting travelers, travel brands, and transport providers worldwide.

## About

Mozio Inc. was founded in 2011 and operates a marketplace that aggregates 3,000+ transport providers across 180+ countries and 3,500+ airports. The platform handles the full booking lifecycle — search, pricing, booking, driver coordination, and post-trip support — for airport transfers, taxis, shuttles, limousines, buses, and hourly rides.

Mozio is rated 4.9/5 based on 17,800+ reviews on Trustpilot.

## Services for Travelers

- **Airport Transfers** — Point-to-point ground transport (taxis, shuttles, sedans, vans, limousines, buses) to and from airports worldwide.
- **Hourly Rides** — Hire a chauffeured vehicle by the hour for flexible city travel and multi-stop trips.
- **Airport Parking** — Compare and book parking options across major airports.
- **Travel eSIM** — International data plans for travelers.

Key guarantees: free cancellation up to 24 hours before pickup, meet-and-greet service, real-time flight monitoring with automatic delay handling, 24/7 customer support, and a network of licensed, vetted drivers.

## Solutions for Travel Brands

Mozio offers white-label and API-based integrations for:

- **Online Travel Agencies (OTAs)**
- **Tour operators**
- **Corporate and business travel platforms**
- **Hotels**
- **Tours & activities platforms**
- **Travel distribution platforms (GDS)**

Integration options include a full white-label product, a REST API, an embeddable search widget, and a travel agent booking tool. Partners get one contract covering global inventory, with flight monitoring and driver coordination handled by Mozio.
